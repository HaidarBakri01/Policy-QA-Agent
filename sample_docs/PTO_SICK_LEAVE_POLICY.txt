PAID TIME OFF (PTO) AND SICK LEAVE POLICY

Section 1: Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual
All full-time, regular employees accrue 15 days of PTO annually. Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rata basis. PTO is accrued monthly and rolls over up to a maximum of 5 unused days at the end of the calendar year.

Section 2: PTO Request Procedure
Employees must request PTO through the HR system (Workday) with at least **7 business days' notice** for all absences of one day or more. Manager approval is required.

Section 3: Sick Leave Entitlement
Employees are entitled to 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Sick leave is available immediately upon hire and does not require accrual.

Section 4: Sick Leave Procedure
For any absence due to illness, the employee must notify their direct manager via phone or messaging (Slack/Teams) **at least 30 minutes before their scheduled start time**. For absences exceeding three (3) consecutive days, a doctor's note may be required by HR.

Section 5: Bereavement Leave
Employees are granted up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the loss of an immediate family member.
