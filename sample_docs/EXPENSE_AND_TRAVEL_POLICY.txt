EMPLOYEE EXPENSE AND BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY

Section 1: Expense Submission
All employee business expenses must be submitted through the Expensify system. Receipts must be attached for all transactions exceeding $25. The submission deadline is **15 days** after the expense is incurred.

Section 2: Meals and Entertainment
Employees are eligible for reimbursement for business meals with clients or external partners. The per-person limit for client meals is $75, excluding alcohol. Meals consumed while traveling alone are limited to a daily maximum of $50.

Section 3: Business Travel
All international and domestic air travel must be booked through the designated corporate travel portal (Concur). Employees are expected to book economy class unless the flight duration exceeds 8 hours, in which case Premium Economy is permissible with prior approval.

Section 4: Mileage Reimbursement
Personal vehicle mileage used for business purposes will be reimbursed at the current IRS rate, provided a mileage log detailing the purpose and destination is included in the expense report.
